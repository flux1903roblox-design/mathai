# Duolingo's learning-experience design (Duolingo Math and the core app), 2024-2026

Scope note: these notes are for a team building a Hebrew (RTL), phone-first math practice app for grades 7-8. Each source is tagged **[Duolingo]** (company blog, whitepaper, shareholder letter, earnings call), **[Independent]** (journalism or academic work), or **[3rd-party/fan]** (fan wiki, UX teardown, style-scraper site, aggregator). Anything older than 2024 is marked **(older: year)**. Research date: Nov 2026.

## 1. Duolingo Math: grades and topics, lesson structure, exercise types and inputs, feedback, adaptivity, place in the path, 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
Duolingo Math started in 2022 as a separate iOS app for elementary and "brain training" math. It moved into the main Duolingo app in 2023 and has since grown into a K-12-style course that follows US Common Core. In Dec 2025 Duolingo added "60 brand-new math units for grades 6 through 9", including graphing. In July 2026 it published an 8th-grade guide covering linear equations, functions, exponents, the Pythagorean theorem, transformations and bivariate data. The design rests on three ideas: (a) concrete-to-abstract sequencing (CRA / concreteness fading) with several representations of the same idea; (b) direct manipulation of virtual tools (number lines with haptic ticks, clocks, protractors, rulers, fraction partitioning, drag-to-group); (c) the core app's gamified shell (bite-sized lessons, per-exercise sound and color feedback, immediate redo of mistakes, end-of-unit interleaved review). The only efficacy evidence is one 2026 Duolingo-authored study: rising 8th graders improved scores by 14% after four weeks of summer use. Sample size and controls were not available to verify.

**Grade levels and topics**

- Coverage is described as grades 2-12 on the US curriculum, with "grade-specific guides, starting at Grade 2 through 8". **[3rd-party, apparently echoing a Duolingo announcement; not verified against the primary post]** — [TheTechStreetNow](https://thetechstreetnow.com/duolingo-expands-its-math-services-for-more-advanced-and-personalized-learning/)
- An Apple App Store editorial story says the new curriculum covers "everything from arithmetic all the way to linear algebra" (seen only in a search snippet). **[Apple editorial]** — [App Store story](https://apps.apple.com/th/ipad/story/id1896629201)
- Curriculum basis: US Common Core State Standards plus NCTM recommendations, OECD and TIMSS recommendations, and academic research on learning progressions. **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf)

**Lesson structure and pedagogy (from the 2023 whitepaper, the most detailed primary source on Math design)** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf) **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]**
- "Each skill has a discrete learning objective with sequenced and varied exercises." Lessons are "bite-sized", and "on average, a lesson takes no more than a few minutes".
- Lessons begin with a "follow the pattern" exercise that pairs concrete representations with less concrete or abstract ones. Later, less-concrete representations appear alongside numerals, and learners "move between these representations" during the lesson. This follows the concrete-representational-abstract (CRA) framework and "concreteness fading".
- Multiple representations for part-whole ideas: "partitioned figures, number lines, number grids, and fraction notation."
- Visual highlighting: for elapsed time, the clock is shaded "from 12:00 to the relevant hour mark." Design rule: "every element on a screen is directly tied to the task or a learning outcome."
- Embodied interactions: moving clock hands; "learners physically slide their fingers along time scale number lines to match the time displayed on an analog clock"; a fraction number line with "haptic feedback at fractional intervals"; for division, "learners can create groups and drag items from the set to ensure they are equal."
- Virtual manipulatives: "protractors, rulers, clocks, number lines, and create fractions through partitioning a variety of shapes." Number-line start and end points, intervals, and the "amount of feedback from sliding across or tapping" can be tuned to learner needs.
- Mistakes: "When learners make a mistake, they have an opportunity to do the exercise again immediately and then at the end of their lesson." A **review skill at the end of each unit** "presents previous mistakes along with other exercises from the unit," which gives mixed, interleaved practice.
- Speed rounds for retrieval practice can be played **timed or untimed**, "to support those who may be negatively impacted by the presence of a timer."
- Feedback: "Correct responses are followed by a pleasant sound and a flash of green." Empowering messages appear "at unpredictable intervals throughout a lesson".
- Anti-anxiety measures: "streamlined exercises with minimal words"; effort-based praise; characters rebuilt as "3-dimensional figures"; "haptics across exercises."
- Streak with "cheerful, math-specific notifications and animations."

**How visuals and exercises are built (engineering)** — [Duolingo blog: Developing Math, Oct 18 2024 (Sammi Siegel)](https://blog.duolingo.com/developing-math) **[Duolingo]**
- In math, "knowledge components" have no consistent form, so visuals change with difficulty and context. Addition appears as countable blocks, integers and word problems. Perimeter "looks different" even though it rests on addition.
- Difficulty scales through both the visuals and the number range, for example a max result rising "from 10 to 100 to 500". There are hundreds of visual variations for countable blocks alone and thousands across domains.
- A `GriddedGraphView` (an iOS CAShapeLayer drawing an M×N grid with masks, lines, buttons and labels) produces area, perimeter, decimal, shape and coordinate visuals. One class can generate thousands of variations, swapping components and colors and adding localized text.
- Real-world contexts: counting money, measuring ingredients, using a ruler, slicing a pizza into equal parts, dragging $50/$20/$5 bills to the center. Interactive challenges include building quadrilaterals and a liquid-volume "wave of water".
- When porting to Android, the team moved hard-to-code visuals to **Rive** ("an animation tool with programmatic inputs"). In-house "Creative Technologists" build the animations, which keeps behavior consistent across platforms.
- A Duolingo Math number-line slider has a custom house-shaped thumb that "snaps dynamically to each tick mark." **[3rd-party motion gallery]** — [60fps.design](https://60fps.design/shots/duolingo-math-slider-2)

**Puzzles and Life Skills (2024)** — [Duolingo blog, Sep 24 2024](https://blog.duolingo.com/math-puzzles-games/) **[Duolingo]**
- "Life Skills" break multi-step real-world problems into steps (planning a trip to Rome with a time change, comparing store discounts).
- Four mini-games: **Cash Dash** (timed money comparisons such as "Is 500 cents better, or 4 dollars?", or ×3/2 versus +$200); **Secret Equation** (deduce a hidden equation in five guesses); **Magic Squares** (3×3 grid where rows and columns sum to the same number); **Math Paths** ("one minute to find as many paths as you can that add up to the target number" on a 4×4 grid). These were iOS-only at launch and reached through a puzzle icon at the bottom of the screen.

### Inferences
- For a grade 7-8 Hebrew app, the 8th-grade unit list maps closely onto the Israeli 7-8 curriculum (linear functions, exponents, Pythagoras, transformations, data). The exercise patterns to borrow are graph-a-line on a small integer grid (-5..5), drag on a number line with snapping and haptic ticks, match a table to its equation and graph, fill in a two-way table, and rotate/reflect a shape.
- Duolingo's math difficulty is mostly parametric: the same template with different number ranges and representations, generated in code. This suits a small team. Build a few templated exercise types with parameters rather than hand-authoring each item.
- Concrete-to-abstract within a lesson (picture, then picture plus numeral, then numeral only) and "immediate redo plus end-of-lesson redo plus end-of-unit mixed review" are the most transferable pedagogy mechanics.
- Offer timed and untimed speed rounds as a choice. Duolingo's own rationale is math anxiety.
- RTL caveat: number lines, coordinate grids and equations stay left-to-right in Hebrew math notation while the UI chrome mirrors. Duolingo publishes nothing on this. It is a team decision (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- I found no primary source listing current Duolingo Math exercise input types (for example, whether there is a custom number pad versus tap-to-select tiles, or how many exercises a math lesson has in 2025-2026). The 2022 "five-minute micro-lessons" figure is the only published length.
- I found no documentation of math-specific wrong-answer explanations (worked solutions, hints). The whitepaper mentions only immediate redo and end-of-lesson redo.
- Whether Energy applies inside Math lessons is undocumented. The fan wiki and Duolingo blog describe Energy generically.
- Full text of the 2026 Math efficacy study (sample size, control group, measure) was not accessible.
- No Duolingo material on RTL or Hebrew rendering of math.

## 2. The core app loop: path, lesson flow, lesson-complete, streak, daily goal, hearts/energy, gems, leagues, quests, badges, friend streaks, notifications and mascot, sound/haptics, Rive

### Takeaway
The loop is: open the app to the **path** (one guided column of nodes grouped into units), then a lesson of about 3-5 minutes and roughly 15 exercises, with a top progress bar, a bottom **Check** button, and a green or red bottom sheet with a sound and a **Continue / GOT IT** button. Combo overlays appear at 5 and 10 in a row. Missed items are re-asked before the lesson ends. A **lesson-complete** sequence shows XP, accuracy and speed cards, then "Claim XP", then quest progress, chest or badge rewards, and the streak extension. Around this sit retention systems: the **streak** (plus Streak Freeze, friend streaks, milestone animations), **daily quests** (3 per day), weekly **leagues** (10 tiers), **gems** as the soft currency, and AI-optimized push reminders voiced by the mascot. In mid-2025 **Energy** replaced Hearts for free mobile users. It costs about 1 unit per exercise rather than 1 per mistake, is earned back through answer streaks, and recharges in about a day.

### Cited Findings

**Home path**
- The path replaced the skill tree for all learners on Nov 1 2022. It is "a path that you'll follow step by step." Lessons are grouped into smaller units, and "a level (circle on the path) is equivalent to 1 crown level of a skill", spaced in sequence "to maximize learning and remembering." **(older: 2022) [Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: new home screen design](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design)
- In 2026, tapping a unit label at the top of the path opens a map of current, completed and upcoming lessons, and completed lessons can be revisited. **[Independent]** — [The Learning Standard](https://thelearningstandard.org/news/duolingo-update-helps-students-review-lessons-but-app-limits-remain)
- The 2023 Literacy app section of the whitepaper states the rationale: lessons go "on a linear path" to give learners "a sense of progress (Yee, 2013)". **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf)

**Onboarding (screen-by-screen capture, content dated Mar 2025)** — [ScreensDesign teardown](https://screensdesign.com/apps/duolingo-language-lessons/?via=growthgems) **[3rd-party]**
- Splash, then mascot intro, course choice, "building your course" loader, attribution survey, knowledge-level cards, motivation, value proposition, and the **daily goal picker** ("Casual" preselected, "I'M COMMITTED" button). Next come a **notification pre-prompt** (mascot speech bubble plus arrow) before the OS dialog, a widget promotion with a press-and-hold tutorial, a "start from scratch" versus placement test choice, then account creation and the paywall (free trial). Onboarding screens use a top progress bar and a full-width green Continue button.

**Lesson flow**
- Captured lesson states: answer, **Check**, then a green banner with praise text ("Amazing!", "Great") and Continue; or, when incorrect, the correct answer and a **GOT IT** button. Continue stays disabled until an answer is chosen. There are full-screen **"5 in a row" / "10 in a row"** combo celebrations, a "lesson review" prompt "to fix missed exercises before finishing", and an exit confirmation warning that XP will be lost ("Keep Learning" / "Quit"). **[3rd-party]** — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/duolingo-language-lessons/?via=growthgems)
- "Each lesson consists of multiple exercises, with a reward — a pleasant sound and a brief celebratory animation from our world characters — coming after each completed exercise. Then, after a session is finished, the learner receives a larger dose of positive reinforcement... unlocked material, XP, or in-game currency." **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf)
- Adaptivity: **Birdbrain** uses data from "more than 1 billion exercises completed daily" to estimate learner proficiency and item difficulty, "pick and sort exercises for each learner and then generates dynamic lessons." If a learner does well, difficulty rises, and missed concepts resurface "at the very end of the lesson." **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf)
- Anxiety handling: everyone starts "with receptive exercises with tappable hints so they can choose their level of scaffolding." **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — same source.
- One illustrative breakdown of a language lesson: about 15 challenges in about 3-5 minutes (multiple choice, then word bank, then typing, then mixed speaking/listening, ending on an easier item). The progress bar "still advances slightly" on wrong answers. **[3rd-party reconstruction, not Duolingo-published]** — [Blake Crosley design guide](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/duolingo)
- Dec 2025: tap exercises became "more forgiving: You can correct small mistakes without penalty." **[Duolingo]** — [Product highlights](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/)

**Lesson complete and rewards**
- Lesson complete shows performance cards for **total XP, accuracy, and speed** (practice shows XP, accuracy, time), with a **Claim XP** button. Then come a Daily Quest progress notice (for example 2/2 with gem counter), achievement badges (for example "Flawless Finisher", "Claim Reward"), and a chest reward (for example 10 gems). **[3rd-party]** — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/duolingo-language-lessons/?via=growthgems)
- Dec 2025: "fresh animations for Streak milestones, Friend Streaks, and lesson‑end screens." Rewards were boosted for completing all three Daily Quests. **[Duolingo]** — [Product highlights](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/)
- XP values change over time and conflict between sources. A fan wiki says a completed lesson awards 20 XP (as of Aug 2024), with a combo bonus of up to +5 XP for consecutive correct answers. Other guides cite 10 or 25 XP. **[3rd-party/fan]** — [Duolingo Wiki: XP](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/XP); [Duoplanet newsletter](https://newsletter.duoplanet.com/posts/has-duolingo-finally-fixed-xp)

**Streak, daily goal, Streak Freeze, friend streaks**
- The streak counts consecutive days with at least one lesson. A new milestone animation raised new-learner D7 retention by **+1.7%**. Allowing **2 equipped Streak Freezes** raised DAU by **+0.38%** "and did not encourage learners to take more days off". A Freeze refill cost 400 gems. 7-day-streak learners are "3.6x more likely to complete their course" (correlational). **(older: Jan 2022) [Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: how the streak builds habit](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)
- **Separating the daily goal from the streak** (any lesson keeps the streak alive) raised the share of new learners' DAU on a streak by **19%**. About a year later, "just over half" of daily learners had a streak of 7+ days, up from about a third. **(older) [Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: improving the streak](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)
- The streak screen (2025 capture) shows a current-streak summary, a social comparison card, a monthly calendar with milestones, a Mon-Sun weekly tracker, a streak-goal picker (7-day card plus commit), a friends tab, and a Streak Freeze explainer. **[3rd-party]** — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/duolingo-language-lessons/?via=growthgems)
- **Friend Streaks**: shared streaks with up to 5 friends. Learners with at least one friend streak are **22% more likely** to complete their daily lesson. **[Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: friends/social features](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-social-features/)
- More than 10M users had a 365+ day streak (Duocon 2025). Other aggregators give 5M or 8M, which I could not reconcile. **[3rd-party summary of a Duolingo event]** — [Quartr Duocon 2025 summary](https://quartr.com/events/duolingo-inc-duol-duocon-2025_3P0xmRPi)
- A one-time "Streak Revival" (June 2026) let eligible users restore their longest streak after three lessons. A secondary report puts participation at 15.4M **[3rd-party, unverified]** — [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/for-its-birthday-duolingo-is-finally-giving-learners-what-they-asked-for). The CEO cited "the Streak Revival last quarter" as a win **[Duolingo]** — [Q3 2026 call](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/04/duolingo-duol-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript)

**Hearts to Energy (2025)**
- Under Hearts, free learners "lost 1 heart per mistake"; beginners were "2X more likely to run out of hearts mid-lesson." With Energy, "each lesson uses a little bit of energy," energy is earned back by getting several answers in a row right, it fully recharges in "about a day" (the same as Hearts), and it can be refilled with a rewarded ad or gems. Bonus energy comes from perfect lessons, adding a friend and chests. **Reviewing mistakes at the end of a lesson costs no energy.** Duolingo says its data shows Energy "helps more learners complete lessons" but gives no numbers. Announced Jul 3 2025 as a test with a small group. **[Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: why we switched to energy](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)
- Fan-documented mechanics: 25 energy at the start; 1 unit per correct answer and 1-2 per incorrect; every 5 in a row triggers a lightning animation with Duo and +1-5 energy; passive recharge +1 per hour; an ad gives +3 or +5 (not available mid-lesson when empty); a full refill costs 750 gems (iOS) or 350 (Android); a chest gives +5; Super/Max get unlimited energy. The web client still used hearts. **[3rd-party/fan]** — [Duolingo Wiki: Energy](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Energy)
- The Q2 2025 shareholder letter calls Energy a "pacing system for free users" replacing Hearts, with room to keep iterating (search snippet). **[Duolingo]** — [Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/0b55110c-2eb9-466d-8549-5459e0851290)
- First reports date from May 12 2025 (CNET, The Verge). **[Independent]** — cited in [Duolingo Wiki: Energy](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Energy)

**Leagues, quests, gems**
- Leagues: **10 leagues** (earlier 5), with Diamond at the top. "Every Sunday (based on your timezone), a new weekly league begins." Learners are matched with people of "similar study habits" in a similar time zone, across all courses. The top 10 in Diamond can enter a **Diamond Tournament** (Quarterfinals, Semifinals, Finals), which does not run every week. Leaderboards are turned off by making the profile private. **(published May 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: leagues](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-leagues-leaderboards/)
- Cohort size of 30 and promotion/demotion cutoffs (for example top 10 promote; 24th-30th demote) come only from third-party guides and vary by league and over time. **[3rd-party]** — [We Got This Covered](https://wegotthiscovered.com/fyi/all-duolingo-leagues-explained/)
- Dec 2025: "You can now see—and compete with—friends in Leaderboards." **[Duolingo]** — [Product highlights](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/)
- Friends Quests: weekly joint quests with a matched or chosen friend. **[Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: friends features](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-social-features/)
- 2026 "Avatar Collections" events (first: "Junior's Toy Hunt", where lessons earn puzzle pieces that unlock outfits) were called "one of our biggest experiment wins" for lesson completion, with regular collection events planned. **[Duolingo]** — [Q3 2026 shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)

**Notifications and the mascot**
- Practice reminders come from a pool of pre-written templates personalized by language and streak. A "sleeping, recovering" **bandit algorithm** picks among them, with the reward being "a learner completing a lesson." New notifications show a "novelty effect" that fades with repetition, so recently seen templates are demoted using a forgetting-curve model. "Time for [language]" works well for Chinese learners but not English learners. Built on about 200M reminders over 34 days. **(older: 2020) [Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: Hi, it's Duo](https://blog.duolingo.com/hi-its-duo-the-ai-behind-the-meme/). The paper (KDD 2020) reportedly showed about +0.5% DAU and more than 2% new-user retention **[secondary, not verified in the paper]** — [Vicki Boykis Substack](https://vicki.substack.com/p/duo-the-push-and-the-bandits); [Duolingo Research](https://research.duolingo.com/)
- Growth rule: "protect the channel." Raising notification volume needed strong justification and CEO approval, with Groupon's email decay as the cautionary example. **(older: 2023) [ex-Duolingo CPO]** — [Lenny's Newsletter, Jorge Mazal](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)
- Q3 2026: "hundreds of experiments, including notifications and streaks" in one quarter; experiments run in the last month were "up about 90% year-over-year." **[Duolingo]** — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [Q3 2026 call](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/04/duolingo-duol-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript)

**Sound, haptics, character animation**
- Haptics are used "in small bursts and low amplitudes," citing evidence they are pleasant (Salminen et al., 2009). Math uses "haptics across exercises." **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf)
- A developer who recreated the Check button observed that the haptic fires on touch-down only, not when a finger leaves and re-enters the button. **[3rd-party]** — [Threads @featherless](https://www.threads.com/@featherless/post/C-L4QNCOUN4)
- Settings include lesson-experience toggles for sound effects, haptics, motivational messages and similar options (seen in a search summary of captures). **[3rd-party]** — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/duolingo-language-lessons/?via=growthgems)
- Characters use Rive for real-time lip-sync from visemes. Duolingo describes "a lot of mouth movements to animate" across its World Characters. **[Duolingo]** — [Duolingo blog: world character visemes](https://blog.duolingo.com/world-character-visemes). Math visuals also moved to Rive for programmatic inputs **[Duolingo]** — [Developing Math](https://blog.duolingo.com/developing-math)

### Inferences
- The minimum viable loop to copy: path node, then a lesson of 10-15 items (about 3-5 min), then the Check / bottom-sheet / Continue rhythm, then required redo of misses at the end, then a lesson-complete stat card (XP, accuracy, time), then the streak tick. Duolingo's own A/B results support these specific details: decouple the streak from the daily goal (+19%), offer up to 2 freezes (+0.38% DAU, no more skipped days), use streak milestone animations (+1.7% D7), and add friend/shared streaks (+22% daily completion).
- For a school-age audience, prefer an Energy-like design that **rewards streaks of correct answers and never charges for reviewing mistakes**, or drop the limiter altogether. In Duolingo the limiter exists to monetize free users (it is a "pacing system for free users"), not to teach.
- Leagues are a strong engagement lever (see Section 4), but with 13-14-year-olds, matching cohorts by "similar study habits" and allowing opt-out are the parts worth copying.

### Gaps
- No primary source for the current (2025-2026) bottom-navigation tab names or icons. Captures confirm Home/path, Leaderboard and Quests screens, plus a puzzle icon for math games (2024).
- No official numbers on Energy's effect on lesson completion, nor on how many exercises a current lesson has.
- No official specs for sound files, haptic patterns or animation durations.

## 3. Concrete UI details: nav, chunky 3D buttons, colors, typography, illustration, motion, celebration staging

### Takeaway
Duolingo's look: flat, saturated colors on white; chunky rounded buttons with a darker 4px bottom edge that "presses" down; one bold rounded custom typeface (Feather Bold) for display text; geometric, rounded-shape characters animated in Rive; and color coding by meaning (green = correct/primary, red = mistake, blue = info/progress, orange = streak, yellow = XP, purple = leagues/premium). Celebrations are layered: small per-exercise feedback (sound plus color flash), mid-lesson combo overlays (5/10 in a row), a staged end-of-lesson reward sequence (stat cards, Claim XP, quest, chest/badge, streak), and rare large milestone animations. Exact token values are published only by third-party scrapers and reconstructions.

### Cited Findings
- The custom typeface **Feather Bold** was created by Johnson Banks and Fontsmith as part of a logo and brand refresh. **(older: ~2019) [Independent]** — [Creative Bloq](https://creativebloq.com/news/feather-bold)
- Color tokens (third-party reconstruction): green #58cc02 (primary/correct), dark green #58a700 (button bottom edge/pressed), blue #1cb0f6 (info/progress), red #ff4b4b (hearts/mistakes), orange #ff9600 (streak), purple #ce82ff (league/premium), yellow #ffc800 (XP/rewards), pink #ff86d0 (events), text #4b4b4b, secondary text #afafaf, track #e5e5e5. **[3rd-party]** — [Blake Crosley design guide](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/duolingo). The same green, dark-green and blue values appear on other style-scraper sites, along with DIN Round as a body or fallback face, 2-3px borders and about 12px button radius **[3rd-party]** — [open-design.ai Duolingo system](https://open-design.ai/systems/duolingo/); [Refero styles](https://styles.refero.design/style/7088d695-362b-4e09-b325-fa8136d4f350)
- **3D pressable button** (reconstruction): flat green fill with a 4px darker bottom border. On press the border goes to 0, the face shifts down 4px and brightness drops to 0.95, over a 100ms transition. Uppercase label, weight about 700-800, slight letter-spacing, 16px radius. **[3rd-party]** — [Blake Crosley](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/duolingo)
- Lesson UI (reconstruction): 16px progress bar with gray track and green fill animating over 300ms ease-out. The wrong-answer sheet is "not scary red", slides up in about 200ms, shows the correct solution with a gentle sound and a GOT IT button. The streak flame idles with a 2s scale/rotate and runs a faster "at-risk" animation in the evening. **[3rd-party; not Duolingo specs]** — [Blake Crosley](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/duolingo)
- Check buttons sit at the bottom of the screen. Correct gives a "cheerful 'ding'" and a "you are correct" banner. **[3rd-party UX case study, older]** — [UsabilityGeek UX case study](https://medium.com/usabilitygeek/ux-case-study-duolingo-ade986ec7450)
- Duolingo's stated principles: "a clean, beautiful design with visually pleasing fonts and colors, integrated with easy to understand affordances, so learners know how they can manipulate items on their screen"; cheerful characters with personalities and backstories deliver "brief supportive messages" during exercises; the "emotional design principle" (Plass & Hovey, 2021); Math characters rebuilt in 3D. **(older: 2023) [Duolingo]** — [Whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf)
- Celebration staging captured in the app: full-screen 5/10-in-a-row overlays; lesson complete (XP/accuracy/speed cards, then Claim XP); quest complete card with gems; badge with "Claim Reward"; chest animation with gem confirmation; streak milestone flame badge. **[3rd-party]** — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/duolingo-language-lessons/?via=growthgems)
- Even the energy combo reward is staged as "a lightning animation... with Duo" every 5 in a row. **[fan]** — [Duolingo Wiki: Energy](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Energy)

### Inferences
- The mechanics to adopt without copying the brand: a raised button with a darker bottom edge that collapses on press (about 100ms) with a touch-down haptic; a semantic color system (choose your own hues, keep green/red for correct/wrong, and give streak, XP and league one hue each); a single rounded, heavy display font. For Hebrew, a rounded heavy Hebrew face (for example Varela Round, Rubik or Heebo in heavy weights) is the nearest equivalent. These are named here as candidates; none of them come from a source.
- RTL: mirror the progress bar fill direction, the bottom-sheet button order, and chevrons and arrows. Keep equations and number lines LTR. Celebrations and cards are mostly symmetric and need little change.
- Keep the layered reward cadence (micro feedback on every item, combo every 5, a staged end screen, rare big milestones) and make every animation skippable or tap-to-advance. Duolingo's own reconstruction shows the end-of-lesson sequence takes several taps.

### Gaps
- Duolingo's old design site (design.duolingo.com) now redirects to the blog's design hub. No official token table, motion timings or illustration rules were retrievable, so all hex and ms values above are third-party approximations.
- No reliable source for 2025-2026 nav bar tabs.

## 4. What Duolingo has published about results (research, experiments, retention/DAU, feature impact)

### Takeaway
Duolingo publishes many engagement results from its own A/B tests (streak, freezes, leagues, notifications, friend streaks), and its user numbers keep rising: about 64.9M DAU in Q3 2026, up 29% YoY. Learning-efficacy evidence is thinner and mostly self-funded. For Math there is a single 2026 study (+14% after four weeks of summer use). Nearly all numbers below are company-reported. The streak-to-completion figures (3.6x and similar) are correlational.

### Cited Findings
- **Scale (Duolingo, company-reported):** Q3 2026 DAU 64.9M (+29% YoY, accelerating from 23% in Q2), MAU 153.1M (+13%), paid subscribers 13.1M (+14%). - **Streak feature results (Duolingo blog):** milestone animation +1.7% D7 retention; 2 equippable freezes +0.38% DAU — [blog, 2022](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit); separating streak from daily goal +19% of new-learner DAU on a streak — [blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak); friend streak gives +22% likelihood of daily lesson completion — [blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-social-features/)

- **2026 levers:** the Avatar Collections event was "one of our biggest experiment wins" for lesson completion; game partnerships (Brawl Stars, Eggy Party) brought "millions of new and returning users"; the share of exercises answerable by speaking has doubled since the start of 2026; IAP revenue fell 38% YoY while subscriptions grew 18%; Duolingo Max is being sunset into Super. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)

- **Language efficacy (context, not math):** Duolingo funds and conducts studies on beginner course outcomes (for example reading and listening outcomes comparable to university semesters). **[Duolingo-funded, peer-reviewed]** — [Jiang et al. 2021, Foreign Language Annals](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/flan.12600); an independent comparison of Duolingo versus classroom for beginner French — [Studies in Second Language Acquisition](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/comparing-the-effectiveness-of-duolingo-classroom-instruction-and-classroom-duolingo-instruction-conditions-on-beginnerlevel-french-language-development/68C0E7E296669798089C84CDC7F3BB9E)

### Inferences
- The engagement levers with published effect sizes are streak decoupling, freezes, milestone animations, friend streaks and leagues. Engagement has repeatedly grown faster than independently shown learning gains, which is why the 2026 strategy explicitly adds "teaching better."
- For a school product, the 14% Math result is a weak benchmark: one study, self-funded, summer use, design unknown. Plan your own pre/post measurement from the start.

## 5. Documented downsides and criticism, and what Duolingo changed in response

### Takeaway
The criticism is consistent and well documented. Most of it is journalism, opinion and user backlash; peer-reviewed work is scarce. The main points: (1) Energy, and Hearts before it, act as a paywall that slows free learners, and Energy charges even for correct answers; (2) streaks cause anxiety and "streak-keeping instead of learning" (easy-lesson farming, XP-boost pressure); (3) leagues are stressful and feed XP-farming; (4) guilt-based mascot notifications; (5) shallow, short practice whose long-term retention experts doubt; (6) the 2022 linear path removed learner choice; (7) the 2025 "AI-first" memo caused a brand backlash. Duolingo responded by softening mechanics without removing them: free mistake review, forgiving tap exercises, streak freezes and a Streak Revival, the option to browse any math unit, the ability to revisit completed lessons, timed or untimed speed rounds, and a "teaching better" priority in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Energy backlash:** users call it a "cash grab" and complain that perfect lessons still drain energy, that there is no opt-out, and that free learning is limited. Some report leaving for less gamified apps. **[fan]** — [Duolingo Wiki: Energy, Reception](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Energy). Multiple petitions ask for Hearts back **[user]** — [Change.org: Hearts, not Energy](https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-hearts-not-energy); [Change.org: restore hearts](https://www.change.org/p/restore-the-hearts-feature-in-duolingo-and-remove-energy). A columnist quit over it **[Independent]** — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/). The most common complaint is that it penalizes skilled learners **[3rd-party]** — [MyEngineeringBuddy review](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/duolingo-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings/). Duolingo itself frames Energy as a "pacing system for free users" (a monetization lever) — [Q2 2025 letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/0b55110c-2eb9-466d-8549-5459e0851290)
- **Streak and XP pressure:** a PCWorld writer describes taking "the easy way out" with easy XP to protect the streak and says the gamification "started backfiring" — [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2924235/why-i-fell-out-of-love-with-duolingo.html). XP-boost multipliers turn study into "a stressful countdown" — [Android Authority: XP boost UX trap](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-xp-boost-ux-trap-3674572). "Streak creep": streak maintenance can crowd out intrinsic motivation — [The Decision Lab](https://thedecisionlab.com/insights/consumer-insights/streak-creep-the-perils-of-too-much-gamification). Duolingo's counterweight is its internal "protect the channel" rule — [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)
- **Shallow practice and limited depth:** "cognitive experts question whether short, gamified lessons help students retain information over the long term"; quick confidence gains from streaks "may not translate into durable, long-term mathematical reasoning"; the app is best seen "as a digital workbook, not a primary teacher." **[Independent]** — [The Learning Standard, Oct 2026](https://thelearningstandard.org/news/duolingo-update-helps-students-review-lessons-but-app-limits-remain). A third-party guide says calculus and linear algebra content is thin ("less depth") — [Nibble blog](https://nibble-app.com/blog/duolingo-math)

- **"AI-first" memo (Apr-May 2025):** the CEO's memo on phasing out contractors for work AI can do drew a TikTok and Reddit backlash, and Duolingo's TikTok reportedly lost more than 400K followers. - **Counter-voices:** some users say the mechanics built real habits ("manipulative but it also worked for me"). — [Lemmy thread](https://lemmy.mariusdavid.fr/post/343754/1136300) **[user]**
- **Changes Duolingo made that address these critiques:** mistake review costs no energy and energy is earned for correct streaks — [blog, Jul 2025](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/); tap exercises let you "correct small mistakes without penalty", and you can "browse and start any [math] unit or topic" — [Dec 2025 highlights](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/); revisit completed lessons via the unit map (2026) — [The Learning Standard](https://thelearningstandard.org/news/duolingo-update-helps-students-review-lessons-but-app-limits-remain); up to 2 streak freezes as "slack" (2022) — [blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit); one-time Streak Revival (2026) — [Q3 2026 call](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/04/duolingo-duol-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript); timed or untimed math speed rounds — [whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_duolingo_method_2023.pdf); leaderboard opt-out by making the profile private — [leagues blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-leagues-leaderboards/). Duolingo did **not** remove Energy and says it is "gradually leaning more into monetization" while preserving the free experience — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)

### Inferences
- For grades 7-8 in a school context, the mechanics most likely to backfire are a monetized life or energy limiter, public weekly leagues with demotion, and guilt-toned notifications. Duolingo's own softening steps point to safer versions: free mistake review, rewards for streaks of correct answers rather than penalties, freezes and repair, opt-out or friends-only leaderboards, a cap on daily reminders, and an encouraging rather than guilt-tripping mascot voice.
- To avoid "streak over learning" (farming easy lessons), tie the streak and XP to quality signals such as accuracy, completing the redo of mistakes, and spaced review items, not raw lesson count. Duolingo publishes nothing showing it does this. It is a design opportunity, not a documented Duolingo practice.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed study specifically measuring streak anxiety or leaderboard stress in Duolingo users, or any study of Duolingo Math's gamification effects on adolescents.
- No Duolingo statement directly acknowledging guilt-notification criticism or changing notification tone was found.
